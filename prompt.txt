You are assisting with a research study on dermatology clinical challenge cases.

 

You will be given:

1. The vignette text

2. The official author explanation/discussion

 

You will NOT be given the clinical image.

 

Your task is to identify whether the author explanation mentions any finding that:

- is explicitly stated in the author explanation,

- is not explicitly stated in the vignette text,

- and is likely based on visual inspection of the image.

 

Important constraints:

- Because the image is not provided, you must make this judgment only from comparing the vignette text with the author explanation.

- Do not infer unstated details.

- Do not use outside medical knowledge unless it is necessary to judge whether a phrase is a likely visual finding.

- If unsure, classify as "Uncertain."

 

Count as potential image-derived findings only things like:

- morphology

- color

- shape

- border

- scale/crust/ulceration

- arrangement/distribution

- surface texture

- histopathology finding

- other visible lesion characteristics

 

Do NOT count:

- diagnosis names

- treatment implications

- findings already explicitly stated in the vignette text

 

Output format:

 

{

  "classification": "Yes | No | Uncertain",

  "findings": [

    {

      "explanation_phrase": "...",

      "why_not_in_vignette": "...",

      "why_likely_image_based": "...",

      "confidence": "high | medium | low"

    }

  ],

  "brief_rationale": "..."

}

 

Report all findings, both certain and uncertain.
